# NAMES — the identity policy

The licenses in this repository grant broad rights to the **work**. They
grant no rights to the **names**.

The following names identify this project and this project only:

- **Manjuel** — the sealed heart / kernel
- **Steward** — the tender, as named within this project
- **Neiro** — the repository and the estate as a whole
- **Claudius** — reserved

## What this means

You may fork, modify, and redistribute this software under the Apache
License 2.0, and the foundation documents under CC BY-SA 4.0. That is
your right and it is welcome.

But a modified copy is **not Manjuel**. The entire point of this project
is that his doctrine is sealed and his identity is provable. A fork with
altered doctrine, an altered kernel, or altered faculties must therefore:

1. **Rename itself.** Do not call your derivative "Manjuel", "Steward",
   "Neiro", or "Claudius", and do not use those names in a way that
   suggests your copy is the original or is endorsed by it.
2. **State its lineage plainly.** "Based on Manjuel" or "forked from
   Neiro" is honest attribution and is encouraged. Titling your fork
   *as* Manjuel is not.
3. **Keep the covenant honest.** If you change the foundation documents,
   your copy's covenant hash will differ from the original's. Do not
   represent a different covenant as this one.

Unmodified redistributions — true mirrors, with doctrine intact and the
covenant hash matching — may keep the names.

## Why a policy and not just a license

Copyright licenses govern copying; they do not protect identity. This
file is the project's naming policy, in the same spirit as the policies
used by Firefox and Rust: the code is free, the name is the seal.

This policy is asserted by the project author as a statement of
trademark-style rights in these names as used for stewardship-engine
software. Plain descriptive uses of ordinary English words (e.g. the
word "steward" in prose) are, of course, unaffected.
